## الغرض والاستخدام | Purpose and use
Tamweel Lite is strictly intended for educational credit-risk modeling using synthetic data. It estimates default risk within 90 days using 22 application-time predictors. A decision of 1 represents a review flag, while 0 represents no flag, not loan rejection or approval. The model must not be used for actual underwriting, automated loan decisions, commercial credit assessments, or regulatory fairness certification.
بيانات Tamweel Lite اصطناعية؛ الهدف حدث خلال 90 يومًا بعد الطلب. 22 خاصية متاحة وقت الطلب؛ لا معرفات أو تواريخ أو هدف في المدخلات. الاستخدام التعليمي فقط؛ لا قرارات تمويل فعلية.
## البيانات والتحقق | Data and validation
حوض التدريب والاختيار: 6576 طلبًا. المعايرة: 836 طلبًا و78 موجبًا. حجز عملاء المعايرة، 90 يومًا لنضج التسميات، وOOF أمامي متداخل مع فصل العملاء في المستويين. طيات المقارنة: 2023Q1 و2023Q3 و2024Q1. نستبعد النتائج التي لم تنضج قبل الأدوار التالية؛ آخر بيانات التدريب لا تستخدم تلقائيًا.
Nested forward OOF validation addresses leakage risks through chronological splits, 90-day label maturity, customer separation, and inner splits for ensemble fitting. The experiment generated 2,155 OOF predictions across three validation periods, covering 32.77% of the fit-and-selection pool and 21.55% of the complete dataset. Limited historical coverage and reuse of OOF evidence for model and threshold selection mean that these results remain development evidence rather than an independent final-test estimate or guarantee of future generalization.
## النموذج والقرار | Model selection
KEEP SINGLE / Logistic. انحراف AP المرجعي: 0.029808. ارجع إلى artifacts/ensemble_comparison.csv وday5_fold_scores.csv للأرقام الكاملة.
Logistic Regression achieved the highest mean AP of 0.39166, with a fold SD of 0.02981, Brier score of 0.06327, and ECE of 0.01882. Equal averaging, weighted averaging, and stacking achieved AP scores of 0.37170, 0.38942, and 0.38314, respectively. None satisfied the required AP improvement greater than the reference fold SD. The worth-it gate also limited Brier and ECE deterioration to 0.005 and 0.01, respectively. Consequently, Logistic Regression was retained because additional ensemble complexity was not justified. This gate is a selection heuristic, not a statistical significance test.
## المعايرة | Calibration
Sigmoid على عينة محجوزة من التدريب والاختيار؛ الرسم والمقاييس تشخيص على عينة تعلم المعاير، وليسا اختبارًا مستقلاً. لا ادعاء بتحسن على تحدٍّ مجهول التسميات.
Sigmoid calibration was fitted exclusively on 836 reserved applications, separate from model training. Brier score increased from 0.07647 to 0.07806, log loss from 0.26649 to 0.27730, and ECE from 0.02112 to 0.03487, while ROC-AUC and AP remained unchanged. Calibration preserved ranking ability but did not improve the reported reliability metrics. The threshold was transported from 0.168922 to 0.122258 without challenge tuning. These metrics are calibration-fit diagnostics, not independent evidence of future probability reliability.
## السياسة والسعة والمناطق | Policy and regions
خسارة 10 FN + FP، عتبة OOF الخام 0.16892161427109176 والمنقولة 0.12225843144286948. سعة الدفعة 300؛ المرشحون 330؛ الإشارات النهائية 300. كتلة الدرجات المتساوية لا تقسم. 1=إشارة مراجعة تعليمية، 0=عدم رفع الإشارة.
The selected OOF threshold produced 245 flags (11.37%) with a loss of 1,111 units, satisfying the 12% capacity ceiling across all validation periods. Of the 2,500 challenge applications, 330 were initially eligible, and 30 were removed by the full-batch capacity policy, leaving 300 flags (12%). Equal-score blocks were handled collectively. Regional OOF FPRs were 7.98% for central (489 negatives), 10.29% for western (486), 6.31% for eastern (507), and 8.10% for other (494), producing a gap of approximately 3.98 percentage points with no low-support warnings. These results are descriptive, not fairness certification, and challenge-level performance cannot be assessed without outcomes.
## التفسير وحدوده | Explanation scope

Day 4's SHAP and permutation importance results explain the weighted LightGBM model, not Day 5's final Logistic Regression model. Although bureau_score and dti ranked highest in Day 4, these findings cannot automatically transfer. Global importance, local contributions, reason statements, and explanation stability must be recomputed for the final frozen model, distinguishing raw scores, calibrated probabilities, and capacity-constrained decisions. Explanations describe model behavior, not causality or fairness.
## المتابعة والقيود | Monitoring and limitations
A hypothetical monitoring framework should track data quality, feature drift, missing values, score distributions, and review volumes against the 12% capacity ceiling. Reference results include mean AP of 0.39166, OOF loss of 1,111 units, and 300 final challenge flags. Once 90-day outcomes mature, monitoring should assess discrimination, calibration, recall, precision, loss, and regional FPR with appropriate support checks. The observed regional FPR gap of 3.98 percentage points is a descriptive reference. Material deviations should trigger investigation and governed corrective actions rather than ad-hoc tuning.
OOF يستخدم للاختيار، وثلاث فترات ليست اختبار دلالة. العتبة قد تتغير سعتها عند نقلها إلى نموذج معاد التدريب. لا تسميات للتحدي، ولا مقاييس أداء أو شهادة عدالة له. البيانات لا تمثل أشخاصًا أو مناطق حقيقية.
